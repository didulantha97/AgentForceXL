# Nordic (Swedish-first) Property Management Systems (fastighetssystem): Competitor Landscape, late 2026

Research date: 2026-11-21. Budget-limited pass (about 17 tool calls). Several sources are vendor-sponsored or comparison sites run by adjacent vendors; this is flagged where it applies. Items with no source are in Gaps.

## 1. Main vendors in Sweden and the Nordics

### Takeaway

The large-landlord segment in Sweden is a duopoly: **Vitec Fastighet** (Vitec Software Group) and **Momentum** (owned by Aareon since June 2022). **Aareon's legacy Incit Xpand is being retired**, which is pushing municipal housing companies to replace it, and that creates an open procurement window. Below the duopoly sits a busy long tail of cloud SaaS challengers (Pigello, TenFAST, Real, Tecta, Hogia DinHyresvärd, Fortvillage, Granna and others) aimed at small and mid-size private landlords. In the other Nordic countries the market is national and fragmented: Visma Tampuuri in Finland, Unik Bolig in Denmark, and Fazile/Fenistra plus Xledger ERP in Norway.

**Norway**

- The most-used systems are Xledger (cloud ERP), Business NXT (Visma), Fenistra and Fazile, according to an accounting firm. Xledger claims "9 av 10 eiendomsselskaper velger" it (vendor claim). Visma Property Solutions AS and LAFT.io (an AI-pitched FDV/property platform) are also present. — [Amesto Accounthouse](https://www.amestoaccounthouse.no/bransjer/eiendom); [Xledger](https://xledger.com/no/bransjer/eiendom/); [Azets](https://www.azets.com/no-no/bransjer/eiendom/eiendomsforvaltning); [Estate Nyheter bransjeguide](https://bransjeguide.estatenyheter.no/okonomisystemer/); [LAFT.io](https://www.fdvbygg.no/index.html)

### Inferences

- Swedish market structure: two enterprise incumbents (Vitec, Aareon/Momentum), one forced-migration pool (Xpand customers), and 20+ SMB SaaS tools. The open middle is mid-size private landlords and multi-client managers (förvaltningsbolag) that have outgrown SMB tools but don't want a Vitec/Momentum project.
- Aareon owns both Momentum and Xpand, so many Xpand migrations will default to Momentum. Contested tenders (Umeå, Karlskrona) show buyers do run open competitions.
- The Nordic markets are national (Tampuuri in FI, Unik in DK, Fazile/Fenistra in NO). No vendor is truly pan-Nordic in residential rent administration. Pigello wants to be, and Visma owns pieces in several countries.

### Gaps

- No verified information found on: Fastigo, Rentalista, Hemnet-adjacent landlord tools, Fastighetsägarna-branded tools, Qimtek, Homepal, "Tenant app", "Bostadsportal", Lessor, EDC (EDC is a Danish real-estate brokerage chain, not a PMS; unverified here), Dedu, or a Swedish "Visma Fastighet" product. **Ecolean** appears to be a packaging company with no PMS relevance (not verified in this pass).
- No independent market-share figures for Sweden. Vitec Fastighet's customer and apartment counts are not published in the sources found.
- Facilit (maintenance/FM, Swedish), Metry (energy data) and Planon were not researched in depth.

## 2. Feature scope (hyresavier, OCR/Bankgiro, Autogiro, Swish, KPI indexation, queue, portal, work orders, metering, owner accounting, SIE, ERP, BankID, GDPR)

### Takeaway

Rent notices (avisering), tenant portals ("Mina sidor"), fault reporting (felanmälan) and Fortnox/SIE integration are table stakes, even in SMB tools. Public feature lists rarely mention OCR/Bankgiro matching or Swish, and Autogiro and BankID appear only in some products. That suggests payments and identity are thinner or less marketed than core leasing, and they are a place to differentiate.

### Cited Findings

- From Planima's 2026 feature matrix (only what vendors publicly list):
  - **Avisering**: DinHyresvärd, Enkla Fastigheter, Fortvillage, Granna, Mowida, Tecta, TenFAST, dinafastigheter, Hogia, Real.
  - **Autogiro**: only Tecta, Strifast (option) and Real (add-on).
  - **BankID**: Fortvillage, Granna, Summera, Tecta, TenFAST.
  - **OCR and Swish**: not mentioned for any system.
  - **Energy/metering**: Vitec, Momentum, Pondus Pro, Pythagoras, Real, Fortvillage, CamsWeb, LEB, Planon.
  - **Fortnox/SIE**: most SMB tools, Pondus Pro and Real (Fortnox and Visma).
  - **Kivra digital mailbox**: Real, Tecta, Fortvillage.
  - Planima notes that a missing tick does not mean the feature is missing.
    — [Planima](https://planima.se/blogg/alla-fastighetssystem)
- Vitec is used daily for felanmälningar, letting apartments and sending hyresavier (Helsingborgshem case). — [Vitec (via search snippet of vitec-fastighet.com)](https://www.vitec-fastighet.com/event-nyheter/nyheter/haninge-bostader-valjer-vitec/)
- Momentum's modules include an API and a built-in inspection (besiktning) tool. — [systemguiden](https://systemguiden.org/jamfor-system/dinafastigheter-vs-momentum)
- Queue (bostadskö): Unik Bolig handles waiting lists in Denmark and integrates with Waitly. — [Waitly](https://waitly.eu/business/cases/waitly-collaborates-with-unik-bolig)
- Vitec bought 80% of Infometric (Swedish energy and water metering for housing; SEK 137.5m 2025 sales). Utility metering and IMD billing are being folded into the incumbent stack. — [TipRanks/Vitec announcement](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/vitec-bolsters-energy-and-real-estate-software-portfolio-with-infometric-acquisition); [Vitec AR 2025](https://storage.mfn.se/a/vitec-software-group/0f7770f2-234f-4832-a654-5d2da3905f56/vitec-software-group-annual-report-2025.pdf)

### Inferences

- With Infometric, Vitec likely has the strongest native utility metering and individual billing (IMD) story in Sweden. A Salesforce product would need a metering-partner integration (Infometric itself is now Vitec-owned, so the partner would be someone else, e.g. Metry) rather than building metering.
- Payments (OCR matching, Autogiro, Swish, Kivra) and BankID are weakly advertised across the market, which is room for a modern product to differentiate.

### Gaps

- No source confirmed which vendors support KPI (CPI) indexation of commercial leases, owner/landlord trust accounting for multi-client managers, deposit handling, or service-charge reconciliation. These are very likely standard in Vitec and Momentum but unverified.
- GDPR features and data residency were not covered by any source found.

## 3. Pricing models, customer base, typical customer

### Takeaway

The enterprise vendors (Vitec, Momentum, Hogia, Pythagoras, Unit4, Planon) price on quote only. SMB tools publish per-object or flat SaaS prices, from free (5 objects) to about SEK 10 per object per month.

### Cited Findings

- **TenFAST**: free up to 5 objects; SEK 10/object/month up to 99 objects; quote for 100+.
- **Tecta**: about SEK 400/month for 10 objects; about SEK 2,500/month for 100 objects; no start fee.
- **Enkla Fastigheter**: SEK 399/month, unlimited objects.
- **Granna**: SEK 9,990 start fee plus a per-object monthly fee.
- **Mowida**: example price SEK 11,580/month.
- **DinHyresvärd (Hogia)**: from SEK 1,800/month.
- **Real**: priced by managed area.
- **Fortvillage**: priced by number of rental objects and companies.
- **Vitec**: cloud packages at 3 service levels, quote only.
- **Momentum, Hogia, Pondus Pro, Pythagoras, Unit4**: quote only.
  — [Planima](https://planima.se/blogg/alla-fastighetssystem)
- Typical customers: Vitec serves allmännyttan plus private residential and office landlords. Momentum serves large listed and private landlords plus allmännyttan (Riksbyggen, Castellum, MKB). Pigello serves private mid-size residential landlords (400–1,800 apartments) and a few municipal companies. — sources in section 1

### Inferences

- A Salesforce managed package carries a per-user licence cost, which doesn't fit the per-object norm (about SEK 10/object/month at the low end). Pricing will probably need a per-object component, or a mid/upper-market position where a quote-based enterprise deal is normal.

### Gaps

- No enterprise price points (for example SEK per apartment per year for Vitec or Momentum) were found. Procurement award documents (tilldelningsbeslut) would hold them.

## 4. Recent 2025–2026 launches, AI, M&A, cloud migration, complaints

### Takeaway

2025–2026 is the AI and ecosystem moment. Momentum declared itself "AI-first" (Momentum Intelligens, with the tenant AI agent Milo). Vitec launched Evo / Evo Connect, an integration and AI-agent hub with a partner programme. Pigello opened a developer portal. Incumbents are moving on-prem customers to their own cloud offerings, and Xpand is being retired.

### Cited Findings

- **Momentum Intelligens** (launched autumn 2025) covers case analysis, communication support, rental insights, and **Milo**, an AI agent for tenant tasks such as felanmälan. Vendor claims: cases resolved 60% faster, and 55% of conversations happen outside office hours. ## 5. Anyone combining property management with hotel, serviced-apartment or long-stay operations in the Nordics?

### Takeaway

No evidence was found of a Nordic fastighetssystem vendor that combines residential/commercial lease administration with hotel or short/long-stay operations. Operators in this space (Forenom, Colive, ApartDirect, Allihoop) appear to run hospitality tools separately. No source was found on which PMS they use.

### Cited Findings

- **Forenom** runs about 9,000 serviced and long-stay apartments in Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Germany and is narrowing its focus to business long-stay. It sold 4 Swedish aparthotel units to **Colive** (hybrid concept "The Nooq", operating from April 2026). — [Forenom](https://www.forenom.com/uncategorized/forenom-sells-four-aparthotel-units-in-sweden-to-colive/); [Forenom about](https://www.forenom.com/about/)
- Other Stockholm co-living and serviced-apartment operators: Allihoop, ApartDirect. — [everythingcoliving](https://www.everythingcoliving.com/coliving-blogs/top-coliving-companies-in-stockholm); [guestlyhomes](https://guestlyhomes.com/blog/best-serviced-apartments-sweden-comparison/)

### Inferences

- A product that handles both lease-based renting (hyresavier, deposits, indexation) and nightly or long-stay bookings (channel manager, e.g. Channex, which this project already integrates) looks like an unoccupied niche in the Nordics. Hybrid operators such as Colive and Forenom, and landlords with corporate-housing units, are candidate segments.

### Gaps

- Which hospitality PMS (Mews, Apaleo, Opera, Guesty) the Nordic hybrid operators use was not verified. Whether Vitec or Momentum offer short-term-let modules is unknown.

## 6. Gaps a modern CRM-based (Salesforce) platform could exploit

### Takeaway

The best openings are: (a) Xpand customers forced to migrate and open tenders at allmännyttan; (b) mid-market and multi-client managers between SMB SaaS and Vitec/Momentum; (c) hybrid lease plus hospitality operators; (d) payments and identity depth (OCR/Bankgiro, Autogiro, Swish, BankID, Kivra), which competitors barely advertise; and (e) CRM-native leasing, sales and tenant service with an open ecosystem. The incumbents are only now retrofitting the open ecosystem.

### Cited Findings

- Xpand retirement is driving tenders (Umeå 2025, Göteborgs stift). — [openprocurements](https://se.openprocurements.com/cpv/48420000/); [Svenska kyrkan](https://www.svenskakyrkan.se/goteborgsstift/nyheter/kommande-byte-av-fastighetssystem)
- Incumbents are only now launching open-API and agent ecosystems (Vitec Evo Connect, June 2026; Momentum AI-first, 2026). — [Förvaltarforum](https://forvaltarforum.se/2026/06/16/fran-system-till-ekosystem-sa-bygger-vitec-for-ai-agenter/); [Förvaltarforum](https://forvaltarforum.se/2026/03/18/momentum-blir-ai-first-later-hela-fastighetsbranschen-folja-med/)
- Swish and OCR do not appear in any vendor's public feature list in the 2026 overview, and Autogiro and BankID appear only sporadically. — [Planima](https://planima.se/blogg/alla-fastighetssystem)
- Lock-in and data-export pain are documented. — [Realtid](https://www.realtid.se/pigello-tar-in-nytt-kapital-vill-utmana-etablerade-fastighetssystem/); [Förvaltarforum Xpand](https://forvaltarforum.se/tag/incit-xpand/)

### Inferences

- **Risks**: Salesforce licence cost compared with per-object SMB pricing; Swedish procurement (LOU) requirements such as data residency, Swedish-language UI and SIE4; incumbent depth in Swedish accounting (förvaltningsredovisning) and IMD metering (Vitec plus Infometric); and Pigello as the agile cloud challenger already targeting the same "open API" message.
- **Differentiation to emphasise**:
  - One platform for leasing CRM, tenant service (Agentforce), maintenance (Field Service) and owner reporting.
  - A multi-client data model for förvaltningsbolag.
  - Hospitality and long-stay alongside leases.
  - Strong Swedish payments (Bankgiro OCR reconciliation, Autogiro, Swish, Kivra, BankID).
  - Pan-Nordic extensibility, since no residential PMS currently spans all four countries.

### Gaps

- No direct evidence of an existing Salesforce-native Nordic property-management product was found. AppExchange was not searched in this pass and should be checked.
- No customer-satisfaction surveys (for example Fastighetsägarna or SABO/Sveriges Allmännytta system surveys) were found.
