# Win Nordic hybrids before fighting Mews

_Competitive brief, 21 Nov 2026. Scope: a Salesforce-native, multi-client (2GP managed package) suite covering hotel PMS, Swedish property management, finance and compliance, staff, and integrations. First client in Sweden (SEK), go-live 22 Jan 2027. Claims marked **[unverified]** come from analyst background or conflicting sources and were not confirmed against a primary source. Claims marked **[V]** are vendor-reported._

No Salesforce-native vendor today credibly sells what this suite plans to be. **Booking Ninjas is the only Salesforce-native hotel PMS, and its public material documents neither night audit nor folios** ([Booking Ninjas](https://www.bookingninjas.com/solutions/hospitality/hotel)). The strong Salesforce hospitality players (Thynk, Amadeus Delphi) only do sales and catering on top of someone else's PMS. Native property apps on AppExchange have 0 to 10 reviews each, and none is Nordic ([sfapps.info](https://www.sfapps.info/top-salesforce-real-estate-apps/)). The real competition is a stack, not a single app: a best-of-breed cloud PMS (Mews dominates the Nordics, with Strawberry's ~230 hotels) plus a Salesforce connector on the hotel side, and the Vitec/Momentum duopoly plus 20+ SMB SaaS tools on the property side. Mews raised $300M in January 2026 and spent 2026 shipping RMS, AI messaging, AR, BI and an embedded channel manager on one data model, so a hotel-only head-on fight against Mews is the weakest place to start. The winnable gap combines three things no competitor offers together: (1) hotel stays and residential or commercial leases in one data model, (2) deep Swedish money flows (SIE4, Fortnox and Visma, OCR/Bankgiro, Autogiro, Swish, BankID), which Nordic vendors barely advertise, and (3) Agentforce agents that work on PMS records with no connector in between. **The most urgent finding is a go-live risk.** Sweden's tightened cash register rules (SKVFS 2021:17) apply from **1 January 2027**, three weeks before go-live. Accommodation is not exempt, and any front-desk sale settled by card, cash or Swish must go through a compliant, registered register unless it is invoiced ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/kassaregister)). The recommended roadmap change: pull the Swedish compliance and finance items forward into a go-live gate and an early R1.2, split R2 into "hotel core parity" and "guest experience", run R3 alongside them, integrate rather than build rostering, payroll, revenue management, metering and Peppol, and move the Agentforce guest and tenant agents out of R6 into the hotel and property releases. Core AI should be included in the price, not sold as an add-on.

## Only one Salesforce-native PMS exists, and its core is unproven

The Salesforce-native field splits into three groups, and only one of them competes for the PMS budget. **Booking Ninjas** calls itself the "first & only full-featured property management system built natively on Salesforce". It lists reservations, invoicing, front desk, a booking engine, rate control and reporting. It does not mention night audit or folios, and accounting appears only as a link to integrations ([Booking Ninjas](https://www.bookingninjas.com/solutions/hospitality/hotel)). Its pricing is the only public Salesforce-native reference point: **Core from $400/month for 1–50 units**, then +$8 to +$5 per unit, with Channel Manager +$100/property, POS +$100/location and door locks +$7.50/door. Implementation is extra, and the page states that "Third-party and Salesforce costs may apply" (same source). Its proof is thin: 4 Capterra reviews, none on G2 ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/144189/Booking-Ninjas/), [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/booking-ninjas)), and its own case studies admit that one hotel project "did not reach production go-live". AI is "separately scoped" rather than shipped. Booking Ninjas also markets itself for rentals and tenant portals, so it is the one vendor that could claim the same hotel-plus-property story. No evidence was found that it does lease accounting or anything Nordic.

**Thynk** and **Amadeus Delphi** are the strongest Salesforce-native hospitality brands, but neither is a PMS. Thynk is a "Hospitality Commercial Platform – powered by Salesforce" covering sales and catering, groups, B2B sales and a booking engine. It integrates with Mews, Protel, Sihot and Oracle, is ranked #2 in the 2026 HotelTechAwards for hotel sales, and joined the Agentforce Partner Network with a Hospitality AI Sales Agent for RFP responses ([thynk.cloud](https://thynk.cloud/), [Hotel Tech Report](https://hoteltechreport.com/meetings-and-events/group-sales/thynkcloud), [Hospitality Net](https://hospitalitynet.org/news/4124787.html)). Delphi is "the hotel sales and catering standard, built on … Salesforce CRM" and is used by Accor and IHG ([Amadeus](https://amadeus-hospitality.com/sales-catering-software/delphi/)). Amadeus also picked Salesforce and Agentforce for a new Hotel Service Center product ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4125958/amadeus-collaborates-with-salesforce-on-next-generation-hotel-service-center-solution)). Both are better treated as partners that sit above our PMS than as rivals. Thynk already connects to four external PMSs, so a Thynk connector is a realistic channel into group-heavy hotels.

The third group is **connectors**: Mews for Salesforce, Hapi-Connect, and Oracle's integration platform. These keep the PMS outside Salesforce. A 2026 API study scored Mews, Apaleo, OPERA Cloud, Beds24 and Cloudbeds at 73.3 or higher for openness ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4134549/the-2026-pms-api-study)), which makes "best-of-breed PMS plus connector" easy for system integrators to sell. Whether Mews for Salesforce is still maintained in 2026 is **[unverified]**.

**Salesforce itself is unlikely to build a PMS.** Its hospitality offering is positioned as Agentforce plus Data 360 pulling data "from PMS to loyalty" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/travel-hospitality-transportation/hospitality/)), and its big 2026 hotel deals sit on third-party PMSs: Minor Hotels, with Agentforce Marketing and Data 360 across 640+ properties ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/ap/news/press-releases/2026/04/09/minor-hotels-unveils-global-data-and-ai-platform-to-power-next-generation-guest-experience/)), and Choice Hotels ([Choice](https://media.choicehotels.com/2026-05-07-Choice-Hotels-International-Unveils-New-Technologies-and-AI-Powered-Solutions-to-Help-Owners-Capture-More-Demand-and-Operational-Excellence)). No hospitality or real-estate industry cloud with a reservation or folio data model was found. This works in our favour. Salesforce's own demo apps (Sunshine Trails, Coral Cloud) show agents booking stays and creating housekeeping cases ([Salesforce sample app](https://www.salesforce.com/data/resources/sample-app-hospitality/)). A native PMS makes that story real without a data-ingestion step. The structural downside is that Salesforce licence costs stack on top of the app price. One secondary source says Agentforce editions launched at $195/$395/$550 per user per month in September 2026 **[unverified, sources conflict]** ([hyphenxsolutions](https://hyphenxsolutions.com/Blog/salesforce-for-real-estate-2026/)).

On the property side, AppExchange mostly offers brokerage and commercial real estate CRMs: Propertybase at $79/user/month with 363+ reviews, and Rethink CRE at $159/user/month with 210+. A long tail of operational apps (REDA, Powerhouse, Wunderbricks, TrackMe) has between 1 and 10 reviews each. Wunderbricks (Netherlands) is the only EU-based native property management vendor found ([sfapps.info](https://www.sfapps.info/top-salesforce-real-estate-apps/), [builtworld](https://builtworld.com/company/wunderbricks)). Integrators still advise keeping rent and ledger in Yardi or AppFolio and using Salesforce only as the CRM ([bkonect](https://bkonect.com/blog/salesforce-for-property-management/)). That is exactly the assumption a native suite with real accounting would overturn.

## Mews has set the 2027 bar for hotel buyers

A Swedish hotel buyer will compare us with Mews, not with Booking Ninjas. **Mews has 15,000 customers in 85 countries [V]** and raised a **$300M Series D at a $2.5B valuation** (Jan 2026) to fund agentic AI ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/01/22/mews-raises-300-million-series-d-2-5-billion-valuation/)). At Unfold 2026 it shipped Mews RMS, Guest Messaging with an autonomous Mews Agent, Automations and Accounts Receivable, on top of BI launched in April ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4132634/mews-unveils-the-operating-system-for-hospitality)). It also embedded SiteMinder as "Mews Channel Manager powered by SiteMinder", with about 3,000 hotels migrating ([Mews](https://www.mews.com/en/press/mews-siteminder-channel-manager-partnership)). It launched Mews POS in Sweden ([Mews community](https://community.mews.com/mews-pos-restaurant-operations-90/mews-pos-is-now-live-in-sweden-3115)). Strawberry (formerly Nordic Choice, ~230 hotels) completed its rollout in June 2024 at a pace of five hotels a week ([Mews](https://www.mews.com/en/press/strawberry-completes-rollout-of-mews-across-portfolio-of-230-hotels)). The largest Nordic group is therefore locked in for years. The rest of the Swedish hotel field consists of OPERA Cloud and Infor at chains, **VisBook** for independents and conference and spa hotels (900 to 1,250 customers, figures conflict; Swedish sales "more than doubled" [V]) ([VisBook](https://visbook.se/about-us/), [Sweeply](https://getsweeply.com/articles/hotel-software-nordic-properties)), and **Sirvoy** for micro properties, which "can feel too basic" for complex hotels ([Lyra](https://lyrasystem.com/en/insights/hotel-and-campsite-pms-comparison/)).

### Marketplaces and AI: we cannot match breadth, but we can match depth on fewer integrations

Mews advertises **1,000+ integrations with "no connection fees"** ([Mews](https://www.mews.com/en/integrations)). A third party credits Stayntouch with 1,400+ ([Chatlyn](https://chatlyn.com/en/blog/top-10-pms-software/)). A 2027 entrant cannot close that gap. It does not need to: Strawberry averages about 15 active integrations per hotel ([Mews](https://www.mews.com/en/press/strawberry-completes-rollout-of-mews-across-portfolio-of-230-hotels)), so a curated Nordic set (channel, payments, locks, POS, accounting, RMS, messaging, BI) plus AppExchange and MuleSoft covers the real need. Two shortcuts give wide reach. **Omniboost** connects Caspeco/Trivec, the dominant Swedish hospitality POS group, to Protel, Shiji, Stayntouch, Infor and Mews ([Caspeco](https://caspeco.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/43000780352-protel-via-omniboost-), [Mews Marketplace](https://www.mews.com/en/products/marketplace/trivec-by-omniboost)). **Seam**, a hardware-agnostic smart-lock layer that Mews' Zentry key product is built on, plus direct links to ASSA ABLOY (Vostio/Visionline) and Salto (Inlet), covers the Nordic lock market ([Mews Marketplace](https://www.mews.com/en/products/marketplace/zentry), [ASSA ABLOY](https://www.assaabloy.com/group/en/news-media/stories/access-stories/strawberry-adopts-room-key-in-apple-wallet-through-assa-abloy-and-zaplox), [Salto Inlet](https://saltosystems.com/en/technology-partners/inlet/)). Channex is a sound channel layer for an ISV: wholesale-only and white-label, with no minimums. Its own pages give inconsistent channel counts (78 vs "400+"), and Nordic hoteliers know SiteMinder and RateTiger better ([Channex](https://channex.io/pricing.md), [TravelDailyNews](https://www.traveldailynews.com/hotels-lodging/sweden-hotels-endorses-erevmax-as-the-preferred-channel-manager-partner/)). Keep the channel layer behind an adapter interface so a second connector can be added later.

The AI that actually shipped in 2025–2026 falls into five categories. The first is guest-messaging autopilots: Mews claims 80% of routine queries are handled ([Mews](https://www.mews.com/en/press/mews-launches-guest-messaging-automations)), Cloudbeds about 81% at one property, and Guesty has ReplyAI Autopilot. The second is staff copilots: Oracle OPERA Cloud Assistant (AI room assignment and rate descriptions, **at no additional cost**) ([Oracle](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/new-ai-capabilities-in-oracle-opera-cloud-supercharge-hotel-operations-2026-06-16/)) and Cloudbeds Ask Signals, still in pilot ([Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com/press/ask-signals-launch/)). The third is revenue agents (Guesty, Mews RMS). The fourth is voice booking (Cloudbeds Engage). The fifth is agent marketplaces and MCP servers: Apaleo's Agent Hub and an MCP server in alpha from Aug 2025 ([Apaleo](https://apaleo.com/blog/apaleo-news/apaleo-launches-mcp-server)), and Guesty's MCP server and Agent Hub (Jun 2026) ([TMCnet](https://www.tmcnet.com/usubmit/2026/06/01/10391753.htm)). Residential vendors are on the same path. Yardi Virtuoso has agents for every multifamily role, and AppFolio Realm-X has "Performers" included on all plans plus a Claude connector ([Yardi](https://www.yardi.com/news/press-releases/yardi-delivers-ai-agents-for-every-multifamily-role-and-workflow/), [AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/newsroom/appfolio-introduces-the-new-realm-x)). Two conclusions follow. Basic AI is now expected and expected to be free, so it cannot be a priced add-on. And "agentic" mostly still means messaging, analytics and pricing. Agentforce acting directly on reservations, folios, leases and work orders in the same org as CRM and service is a more substantive claim than any competitor can make, provided concrete agents ship in 2027 rather than as an R6 promise.

## Swedish property is a duopoly with a forced-migration window

Large Swedish landlords run **Vitec Fastighet** (customers are largely allmännyttan, the municipal housing companies, such as Helsingborgshem and Haninge Bostäder) or **Momentum**, which Aareon has owned since 2022. Momentum has about 200 customers, including Riksbyggen, Castellum and MKB ([Planima](https://planima.se/blogg/alla-fastighetssystem), [Vitec](https://www.vitec-fastighet.com/event-nyheter/nyheter/haninge-bostader-valjer-vitec/), [Momentum](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/momentum/pressreleases/momentum-och-avy-ingaar-strategiskt-partnerskap-3323471)). The window: **Aareon is retiring Incit Xpand**, and Umeå's 2025 tender states "Aareon kommer avveckla systemet Incit Xpand" ([openprocurements](https://se.openprocurements.com/cpv/48420000/)). Its customers must re-tender under LOU (public procurement). Many will default to Momentum, but Umeå and Karlskrona show that open competitions do take place. Below the duopoly, **Pigello** (200+ customers, 200,000+ objects, 35+ integrations including Fortnox and Xledger, and an explicit ambition to "bli störst i Norden") is the agile cloud challenger already using the "open API" message ([Pigello](https://www.pigello.se/), [Mynewsdesk](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/pigello/pressreleases/fastighetssystemet-pigello-vill-bli-stoerst-i-norden-fyller-paa-kassan-och-lanserar-marknadsplats-foer-integrationer-3184078)). It sits alongside TenFAST, Tecta, Real, Hogia DinHyresvärd and about 15 others. The other Nordic markets are national: Visma Tampuuri claims about 70% of Finland [V, older], Unik Bolig claims more than 1M units in Denmark, and Norway runs on Xledger, Fazile and Fenistra ([Kiinteistölehti](https://www.kiinteistolehti.fi/visma-vahvistaa-asemaansa-kiinteistohallinnan-ohjelmistoratkaisuissa-ostaa-tampuuri-liiketoiminnan), [proptechwiki](https://proptechwiki.pages.dev/companies/unik-system-design), [Amesto](https://www.amestoaccounthouse.no/bransjer/eiendom)). **No residential system spans all four Nordic countries.**

The incumbents are retrofitting exactly the story Salesforce offers by default. Momentum declared itself "AI-first" with Momentum Intelligens and the tenant agent Milo (claims: cases resolved 60% faster [V]), and gave the AI away free until June 2026 ([Förvaltarforum, sponsored](https://forvaltarforum.se/2026/03/18/momentum-blir-ai-first-later-hela-fastighetsbranschen-folja-med/)). Vitec launched Evo Connect, an integration and AI-agent hub with a partner programme, in June 2026 ([Förvaltarforum, sponsored](https://forvaltarforum.se/2026/06/16/fran-system-till-ekosystem-sa-bygger-vitec-for-ai-agenter/)). Vitec also bought 80% of Infometric, which folds energy and water metering (IMD, individual metering and billing) into its stack ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/vitec-bolsters-energy-and-real-estate-software-portfolio-with-infometric-acquisition)). A tenant chatbot is therefore no longer a differentiator on its own, and utility billing should be bought through a partner, not built. The market is large enough to matter: about **1.6 million rental apartments**, of which about **846,000 are public housing** ([SCB](https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/boende-bebyggelse-och-mark/bostader-och-boende/bostadsbestand/pong/statistiknyhet/bostadsbestandet-31-december-2025/), [Boverket](https://www.boverket.se/sv/samhallsplanering/bostadsmarknad/bostadsmarknaden/bostadsmarknadsenkaten/kommunernas-verktyg/allmannyttan/)). Public housing carries a **SEK 686bn maintenance backlog** ([Sveriges Allmännytta](https://via.tt.se/pressmeddelande/4445474/allmannyttans-underhallsskuld-uppgar-till-686-miljarder-kronor?lang=sv)), which argues for strong work-order and maintenance features. Selling to public housing means LOU tenders and Peppol invoicing.

The hybrid segment is real but unserved. Forenom runs about 9,000 serviced and long-stay apartments across five countries and sold four Swedish aparthotel units to Colive's hybrid "The Nooq" concept, operating from April 2026 ([Forenom](https://www.forenom.com/uncategorized/forenom-sells-four-aparthotel-units-in-sweden-to-colive/)). No Nordic vendor was found that combines lease administration with nightly or long-stay booking. Which PMS these operators use today is **[unverified]**.

## January 2027 compliance creates go-live risk

**The Swedish cash register rules are the top risk.** From 1 January 2027, every cash register in use must meet SKVFS 2021:17. It must be manufacturer-declared, connected to a control unit or cloud control system, registered with Skatteverket before use, and able to export its journal as XML. Card, cash and Swish sales must all be recorded ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/kassaregister)). Skatteverket does not certify registers. The manufacturer is responsible, and inspectors can arrive unannounced and ask for control codes per receipt ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/kassaregister/tillsynsochkontrollbesok)). Accommodation is not on the exemption list. **Invoiced sales are exempt** ("kassaregisterkravet inte gäller fakturerad försäljning"), as are businesses whose cash and card sales are at most SEK 236,800 a year ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/kassaregister/vissaverksamheterarundantagnafrankravetpakassaregister)). Two points remain **[unverified]**. First, whether Skatteverket treats a hotel folio settled by card at reception as a cash-register sale or as a cash invoice. Second, how a cloud "kontrollsystem" under SKVFS 2020:9 applies to a Salesforce architecture. Skatteverket also opened a review of the rules on 16 March 2026 ([Skatteverket PDF](https://skatteverket.se/download/18.70685bee19c85dd5dd02aeb/1773751310702/utredning-om-en-oversyn-av-reglerna-om-kassaregister.pdf)). Becoming a manufacturer-declared register is a regulated product of its own and cannot be delivered by 22 January. **Before go-live the team should do three things.** (a) Route all counter and F&B sales through a certified third-party POS such as Caspeco/Trivec via Omniboost, and import its Z-reports. (b) Design front-desk settlement as invoice-based, so the folio becomes a compliant invoice or cash invoice. (c) Get a written opinion from a kassaregister specialist or Skatteverket on the exact flow. If the first client is a landlord only, rent is invoiced and outside the rules. Whether municipally owned housing companies are exempt as tax-exempt entities is **[unverified]**.

Several other items need to be right on day one. **VAT** must be effective-dated per line. Accommodation and eat-in restaurant service are taxed at 12% ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/servicelankar/otherlanguages/inenglish/businessesandemployers/declaringtaxesbusinesses/vat/vatratesongoodsandservices.4.676f4884175c97df419255d.html)). Food and takeaway are at a temporary 6% from 1 April 2026 to 31 December 2027, then revert ([Riksdagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/en/news/articles/2026/feb/25/temporarily-reduced-vat-on-food_cmsd274f5ec-c794-4c1f-8eaf-7811a27dfcb5en/)). A hard-coded rate will break on 1 January 2028. **Accounting hand-off** matters because Fortnox dominates (about 612,000 customers, an estimated 37% share), with Visma Spiris second and Business Central, Unit4 and Xledger above them ([Realtid](https://www.realtid.se/bors-finans/68-procent-upp-fortnox/), [Chift](https://www.chift.eu/blog/the-accounting-software-landscape-in-sweden-a-2026-guide-for-saas-vendors)). A SIE4 export of daily summarised journals, driven by GL mapping rules (product to account, VAT code and cost centre), is the cheapest universal hand-off. That it suffices for any Swedish system is **[unverified]** but widely assumed. **Channex** requires testing, certification and OTA account authorisation before going live ([Channex docs](https://docs.channex.io/llms-full.txt)). Missing that is a schedule risk if any certification step is still open. **Payments** should be tokenised by the payment provider, with no card numbers stored in Salesforce, to stay within PCI SAQ A/A-EP scope **[unverified background]**. Bokföringslagen's seven-year retention drives the audit-trail and immutable-folio design **[unverified background]**.

Some items can wait. **Sweden has no tourist tax.** Norway's municipal 3% levy starts in 2027 in places like Tromsø, and Finland's 2–5% bill is pending ([Visit Norway](https://www.visitnorway.com/travel-trade/visitor-contribution-accommodation-tax/), [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/europe/finland/experiences/news/finland-tourist-tax-proposal-could-raise-travel-costs-for-visitors)), so a generic "percentage per municipality" engine is a Norway-client trigger, not a 2027 Sweden need. **Peppol** is mandatory only for invoicing the Swedish public sector. EU cross-border B2B e-invoicing (ViDA) follows on 1 July 2030, and a Swedish domestic B2B mandate is under inquiry with a report due 30 November 2027 ([ecosio](https://ecosio.com/en/compliance/sweden/e-invoicing/), [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/services/tax/indirect-taxes/sweden-vat-in-the-digital-age-vida), [Comarch](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/sweden-launches-official-inquiry-into-mandatory-e-invoicing-implementation/)). Use an access point through Fortnox, Visma, Pagero or InExchange rather than building one. NIS2 (the Swedish Cybersecurity Act, in force 15 January 2026) probably does not cover hotels **[unverified]**, but it will show up in procurement security questionnaires ([Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/se/sv/services/legal/perspectives/nis2-now-in-force.html)).

**Staff management is a crowded, mature category led by Nordic vendors.** Quinyx, Planday (owned by Xero), Caspeco, Personalkollen and Tamigo already handle Swedish collective agreements and payroll exports ([Quinyx](https://www.quinyx.com/workforce-management/integrations), [Planday](https://planday.com/integrations), [businesswith.se](https://businesswith.se/jamfor-system/quinyx-vs-caspeco-personal/)). Building rostering, time and attendance, and payroll would compete with specialists while adding no differentiation. The part worth building is the PMS-adjacent operational layer: housekeeping and maintenance checklists driven by room status, plus offline mobile. Standalone tools such as hotelkit (~$200/month) or Flexkeeping (~$400/month) [3rd party figures] sell exactly this, and they need a separate login and a PMS integration ([HotelTechReport](https://hoteltechreport.com/compare/flexkeeping-housekeeping-vs-hotelkit-housekeeping)).

## Reorder the roadmap around Swedish money flows and hybrid operators

### Where the suite can win

Five segments stand out where the evidence shows a real gap. The first is **hybrid hotel, aparthotel and long-stay operators plus landlords with corporate-housing units** (the Forenom and Colive type), which no Nordic system serves on one data model. The second is **mid-size private landlords and multi-client managers (förvaltningsbolag)** that have outgrown TenFAST or Tecta but don't want a Vitec or Momentum project. The third is **Xpand-replacement tenders**, if Peppol, Swedish-language UI, SIE4 and data residency can be shown. The fourth is **independent conference, spa and group-heavy hotels** (VisBook's base), where groups, B2B accounts, city ledger and CRM in one org beat Mews and Cloudbeds' weak corporate billing. The fifth is **Salesforce-committed hotel groups** that currently pay for a PMS, a connector and Data 360 ingestion. Avoid head-on bids for Mews-locked chains such as Strawberry in 2027, and partner with Thynk or Delphi for sales and catering rather than rebuilding it.

### Build vs integrate

**Build** what is unique to running both stays and leases in one Salesforce org: the reservation, folio and night-audit core, city ledger on Accounts, groups, the lease, rent notice and deposit lifecycle, Swedish bank-file reconciliation (OCR/Bankgiro, Autogiro), GL mapping and SIE4, operational checklists and offline mobile, and the Agentforce actions over these objects. **Integrate** commodity or regulated layers: channel management (Channex), card acquiring (Adyen, Nexi, Stripe), POS and kassaregister compliance (Omniboost to Caspeco/Trivec), locks (Seam, Vostio, Salto), accounting ledgers (Fortnox, Visma), Peppol (access point), rostering and payroll (Quinyx, Caspeco, Planday), revenue management (an RMS partner), metering (a partner), and sales and catering (Thynk or Delphi). That list also forms the curated "15 integrations that matter" for the AppExchange listing.

### Pricing and positioning implications

Price per unit, not per user. Hotels are priced per room and Swedish landlords per object (SEK 10/object/month at the SMB low end), while Salesforce's per-user model fits neither. Booking Ninjas' $400/month floor plus per-unit steps shows the format works on AppExchange. Bundle the channel manager (Channex costs about $7/hotel/month) and **core AI** (messaging agent, copilot, tenant agent) into the base price, as Oracle, AppFolio and, until mid-2026, Momentum did. Pass usage-heavy agent work (voice, high-volume messaging) through as Agentforce consumption. Publish prices: the whole category is quote-only or estimated, so transparency costs little and stands out. Be **payment-provider-agnostic** with no payment margin, as a direct answer to Mews' master-account model. The hard problem is the **total cost including Salesforce licences**, since buyers will compare against €8–25 per room per month all-in. Address it with an OEM/embedded licence (an Experience Cloud or platform-only user type for front-desk and housekeeping staff), so that users in clients without existing Salesforce licences do not each need a full CRM licence. The exact licence economics are **[unverified]** and should be priced with the Salesforce ISV team before editions are fixed in R1.1. Position the product as **"the Nordic operating system for operators who run both beds and leases, on the CRM they already own"**, with Swedish compliance, Swedish money flows and named Swedish-language support as the proof points. Do not position it as "a Mews alternative on Salesforce".

## Conclusion

The research changes the question from "how do we catch Mews" to "which ground can't Mews or Vitec follow us onto quickly". Mews is optimising a hotel-only operating system and making money from payments. Vitec and Momentum are adding AI and APIs to lease administration. Neither has a reason to merge stays and leases, adopt Swedish bank rails, or run inside a customer's CRM. Those three overlaps are this suite's moat, and each is cheaper to build on Salesforce than to copy elsewhere. The flip side is that hotel-only feature parity is a cost of entry, not a reason to buy, so R2 should be sized to reach parity and then stop competing feature by feature.

The 1 January 2027 cash register deadline shows that, in this market, compliance is the product. Shiji sells "full fiscal compliance in 36 countries" for the same reason ([Shiji](https://www.shijigroup.com/press-news/shijis-daylight-pms-expands-across-449-cities-and-36-countries-with-full-fiscal-compliance)). A suite that proves it handles the kassaregister rules, SIE4, effective-dated VAT, Peppol and BankID for its first Swedish client turns a go-live risk into its strongest reference. That is the asset that wins the next ten Nordic deals, more than any single feature in R2 or R6. Three facts should be confirmed before the roadmap is locked: the first client's segment (hotel, landlord or hybrid) and its existing POS, lock and accounting stack; a written kassaregister ruling on folio settlement; and Salesforce OEM licence pricing.
